## Title

Bitcoin Son Dakika — Turkish Bitcoin‑Only News on Nostr

## Project Identifier

turkish_bitcoin_news

## Objective (one-liner)

Fund Turkish Bitcoin-only journalism, articles, and podcasts on Nostr with value‑for‑value.

## Story

Bitcoin Son Dakika (https://bitcoinsondakika.com) is a Bitcoin‑only, Turkish‑language media project built on Nostr. Our mission is to make high‑signal Bitcoin education and reporting accessible to everyone in Türkiye —free from altcoin noise, paywalls, and platform lock‑in. We publish succinct breaking news, original reporting, long‑form articles, and podcasts, and we are preparing structured education programs for universities and communities.

Why now? Turkish speakers are one of the most active Bitcoin communities globally, yet credible, Bitcoin‑only Turkish content is scarce. We aim to bridge that gap by producing and curating high‑quality materials in Turkish and distributing them natively on Nostr and the open web. Nostr isn’t just a social protocol for us —it’s how we publish, build community, and monetize via Value‑for‑Value (V4V) using Lightning zaps.

# What we will produce

- Daily: breaking news roundups and curated link posts
- Weekly: original articles, translations of high‑impact Bitcoin pieces, and explainers
- Ongoing: interviews and podcasts with Bitcoiners, builders, and educators
- Upcoming: education tracks for students and newcomers (modules, workshops, study groups)

# How your support will be used

- Commission independent Turkish journalists and editors
- Fund student contributors and translators through micro‑grants and bounties
- Cover production for articles, podcast editing, and transcription
- Support infrastructure, moderation, and community engagement on Nostr

# Accountability and milestones

- Target output: 12–16 articles/month, 4 podcast episodes/month
- Public backlog and roadmap; monthly transparency reports (budget, deliverables)

# Call to action

Support us with sats and help bootstrap a durable, censorship‑resistant Turkish Bitcoin media hub. With your help, we’ll fairly compensate contributors, bring new talent into Bitcoin media, and grow a Nostr‑native community around truth‑seeking Bitcoin journalism.
